Questions buyers will ask

Confidence before checkout.

Are the perfumes authentic?＋
For the real launch, authenticity should be supported by supplier invoices, importer details where relevant, sealed-product photography and a clear returns process. This demo intentionally does not make an unsupported authenticity claim.
Can customers choose by notes instead of brand?＋
Yes. The catalogue data already supports search by oud, musk, leather, vanilla, saffron, citrus and more. Shopify tags and Search & Discovery can mirror this.
Can this become a real Shopify store?＋
Yes. The front-end structure is intentionally Shopify-like: collections, product cards, quick view, cart, product metadata, trust blocks, FAQ and checkout flow. The next step is translating the theme into Liquid sections and importing the approved catalogue.
